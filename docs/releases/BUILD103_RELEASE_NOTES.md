# NRHIS Sprint 2 Build103 Release Notes

Build103 separates genuine high-flow routing coherence from weak low- and medium-flow lag optimizer results in the lower Nueces network.

It scans increasing upstream-discharge thresholds, identifies a sustained descriptive coherence onset only when multiple consecutive thresholds meet the configured Pearson-r criterion away from lag-search boundaries, reclassifies the Build102 flow regimes by correlation strength, analyzes coherent-flow events, and recalculates downstream-minus-upstream residual summaries only within statistically coherent conditions.

The build does not interpret weak optimizer lags as travel times, does not treat residuals as a reach water balance, and makes no causal attribution. Analysis remains local-only with zero USGS requests and no finalized-archive mutation.
