# NRHIS Sprint 2 Build100 Release Notes

Build100 turns the reconciled Rincon evidence produced by Builds097–099 into a formal, reproducible evidence report. It writes a Markdown report, a machine-readable findings table, and a SHA-256-bound report receipt from the latest Build099 local analysis outputs.

The report explicitly separates observed and derived findings from periods where directional discharge is not observable. It does not estimate reverse-flow volume during the September–October 2017 discharge outage or after the May 12, 2018 terminal discharge observation, and it does not infer the cause, source, or destination of measured reverse-direction flow.

Build100 also clarifies a legacy Build099 field name by reporting the terminal row's 3.07 cfs value as the final observed discharge, not as a reverse-flow mean. The upstream Build099 evidence remains unchanged.
