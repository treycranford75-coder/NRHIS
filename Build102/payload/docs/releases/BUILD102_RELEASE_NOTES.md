# NRHIS Sprint 2 Build102 Release Notes

Build102 tests whether the strong Build101 lower-Nueces lag pattern is stable through time and flow conditions rather than being driven by a single correlation maximum or a few large hydrographs.

It adds objective near-peak lag windows, monthly estimates, rolling 30-day and 60-day estimates, upstream-flow-tercile lag estimates, and a descriptive additivity check across Mathis, Bluntzer, and Calallen. It also creates lag-adjusted downstream-minus-upstream residuals for anomaly screening.

NRHIS does not interpret these lags as proven physical water-particle travel time, and it does not interpret the residuals as a reach water balance or assign them to groundwater, diversions, tributaries, evaporation, or other causes. The analysis remains local-only with zero USGS requests and no finalized-archive mutation.
